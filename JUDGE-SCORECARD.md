Pairwise on criteria 1–4, A1 beats every other candidate:
- Criterion 1: all pass (ghost mark + make.fun name). Final covers
  it three ways — drawn ghost character (white ghost, sparkle eyes,
  magenta star coin, magenta halo disc as in the press-kit mark),
  the name make.fun in the caption, and the ghost's own pitch.

  visibly aged at a bus stop, wall of torn calendar pages behind

  the single most GTA-6-specific fact, plus Vice City neon/palms.

  "earn while you wait!" sells make.fun inside the joke itself:
  waiting is the problem, earning on make.fun is the answer.
